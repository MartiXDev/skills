## Source and method notes

- Microsoft Learn pages expose `original_content_git_url` and commit metadata in
  their page headers. Those links were retained as provenance but the canonical
  Learn pages were treated as the evidence surface.
- The connector TOC was fetched directly and filtered by the `custom-connectors`
  path prefix. This avoids treating the thousands of individual prebuilt
  connector pages as custom-connector guidance.
- The current pages contain some legacy links and contradictory limits or
  timelines. The synthesis records those conflicts instead of normalizing them
  into one unsupported value.
